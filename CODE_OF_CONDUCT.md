# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must maintain a respectful, technically constructive environment. Be welcoming, assume good faith, accept correction, focus criticism on the work, and protect other users' privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and whether the participant takes responsibility and changes their conduct. Reporters and affected participants will be treated as confidential to the extent practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and uses a shorter project-specific enforcement process.
